    Admissions

    Apply for Senior 1 to Senior 6. Fill in the form and send it by email or WhatsApp, or call the records office. We confirm dates, fees and requirements when we reply.

    1. Complete the application form.
    2. The records office contacts the parent or guardian.
    3. Bring the student’s birth certificate, last report card and passport photos.
    4. Receive the admission decision and joining instructions.
